About the app

Write notes and checklists, colour and label them, pin what matters and get reminded on time. Autosave means nothing is ever lost.

Features

  • Notes and checklists
  • Colours, labels and pinned notes
  • Reminders at a set time
  • Archive and trash
  • Search across every note
  • App lock and backup

Your privacy

No account and no sign-in. What you create stays on your phone. The app shows ads from Google AdMob, which is the only reason it uses the internet. Read the full privacy policy.
