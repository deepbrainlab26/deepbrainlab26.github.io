About the app

Made for online forms that only accept small photos. Shrink photos to 20 KB, 50 KB, 100 KB or any size, turn photos into a PDF, and compress, merge or split PDFs.

Features

  • Resize to an exact file size
  • Exact pixels or percentage
  • Photos to one PDF
  • Compress PDFs to a target size
  • Merge, split and PDF to JPG
  • Works offline, originals untouched

Your privacy

No account and no sign-in. What you create stays on your phone. The app shows ads from Google AdMob, which is the only reason it uses the internet. Read the full privacy policy.
