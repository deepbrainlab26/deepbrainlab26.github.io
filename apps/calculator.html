About the app

A clean everyday calculator plus money, health and conversion tools: EMI, GST, SIP, FD, RD, percentages, dates, BMI, age and more than a hundred units.

Features

  • Live answers, history and memory
  • Scientific mode
  • Loan EMI with prepayment and compare
  • GST with the CGST / SGST split
  • SIP, FD and RD growth
  • Unit, date, age and BMI tools

Your privacy

No account and no sign-in. What you create stays on your phone. The app shows ads from Google AdMob, which is the only reason it uses the internet. Read the full privacy policy.
