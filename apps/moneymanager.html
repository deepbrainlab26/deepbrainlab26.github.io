About the app

A private expense tracker. Log spending in seconds, set budgets by category, plan savings goals and see clear monthly insights. Everything stays on your phone.

Features

  • Fast entry with categories and accounts
  • Budgets with progress bars
  • Monthly insights and category charts
  • Savings goals with a monthly plan
  • Recurring bills with reminders
  • CSV and JSON backup, PIN lock

Your privacy

No account and no sign-in. What you create stays on your phone. The app shows ads from Google AdMob, which is the only reason it uses the internet. Read the full privacy policy.
