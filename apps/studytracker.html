About the app

A daily study planner for school, college and competitive exams. Set a daily goal, plan topics by subject, time your sessions and watch your streak grow.

Features

  • Daily goal ring and study streak
  • Subjects, topics and exam countdowns
  • Spaced revision after 1, 3, 7, 15 and 30 days
  • Focus timer and daily habits
  • Weekly charts and a shareable progress card
  • Backup and restore, no account needed

Your privacy

No account and no sign-in. What you create stays on your phone. The app shows ads from Google AdMob, which is the only reason it uses the internet. Read the full privacy policy.
