About the app

Scan with the camera or from a photo, see smart results for links, Wi-Fi, contacts and payments, and create your own QR codes. History stays on your phone.

Features

  • Camera, gallery and batch scan
  • Smart results for Wi-Fi, links, UPI and contacts
  • Shows the full link before you open it
  • Create QR codes and barcodes
  • Searchable history with favourites
  • No account, no uploads

Your privacy

No account and no sign-in. What you create stays on your phone. The app shows ads from Google AdMob, which is the only reason it uses the internet. Read the full privacy policy.
