About the app

Take photos with the address, coordinates, date and time printed on them, or stamp a photo you already have. Useful for site visits, field work and reports.

Features

  • Address, coordinates, date and time stamp
  • Several stamp templates
  • Stamp an existing photo
  • Location saved in the photo details (EXIF)
  • Gallery grouped by date and place
  • Location used only for the stamp

Your privacy

No account and no sign-in. What you create stays on your phone. The app shows ads from Google AdMob, which is the only reason it uses the internet. Read the full privacy policy.
