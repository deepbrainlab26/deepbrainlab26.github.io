Study Tracker

Plan topics, keep your streak, ace your exams.

A daily study planner for school, college and competitive exams. Set a daily goal, plan topics by subject, time your sessions and watch your streak grow.

What it does

Study Tracker turns a syllabus into a plan for today, then keeps count of the minutes you actually study.

How It Works

Everything sits in one simple flow: a backlog of topics you have not done, a plan for today that you pull from it, and a done list. Anything you do not finish moves to tomorrow by itself, so a missed day never loses your work.

Start Without Typing

  • Ready-made course packs for school, college and competitive exams: pick one and your subjects and chapters are filled in
  • Or add your own subjects and topics, in whatever way suits your course
  • Nothing is added behind your back. You choose what today looks like

Study Sessions

  • Start a session on a topic and the minutes are logged against it
  • A focus timer with work and break rounds, for the sessions where you need pushing
  • A notification when the round ends, so you can put the phone down

Stay On Track

  • A daily goal ring that fills as you study
  • A streak that counts the days in a row you hit your goal
  • Exam countdowns, so you always know how long is left
  • Daily habits alongside the studying, ticked off as you go

Revision That Comes Back To You

Finished topics return for revision after 1, 3, 7, 15 and 30 days. Spacing the repeats out is what moves something into long-term memory, and the app does the remembering for you.

See The Progress

  • Weekly charts of minutes and topics
  • A progress card with your name on it that you can share
  • Subject-by-subject totals, so you can see what you have been avoiding

Your Work Stays With You

Subjects, topics and every study minute live in this app on your phone. There is no account and no sign-in, and nothing is uploaded. Backup and restore write a file you choose, so moving to a new phone is one step.

Study Tracker is paid for by ads, and asks for your consent where the law requires it. You can change that choice at any time in Settings.

QRMate - QR Scanner & Generator

Scan and create QR codes and barcodes.

Scan with the camera or from a photo, see smart results for links, Wi-Fi, contacts and payments, and create your own QR codes. History stays on your phone.

What it does

QRMate scans QR codes and barcodes with your camera, and makes your own codes when you have something to share.

What You Can Scan

  • QR, Data Matrix, Aztec and PDF417
  • Barcodes: EAN-13, EAN-8, UPC-A, UPC-E, Code 128, Code 39, Code 93, ITF and Codabar
  • Torch, pinch or slider zoom, tap to focus, and the front camera when you need it
  • Light codes on a dark background read as easily as the usual dark on light
  • Scan a picture you already have, chosen with the system photo picker. When a picture holds several codes, you choose which one to open
  • Batch scan collects code after code in one session, each one only once, then copies or shares the whole set as CSV

What Happens After A Scan

QRMate works out what kind of code it is and offers the sensible next step:

  • Links open in your browser, after the safety check below
  • Wi-Fi codes hand over to Android's own save-network sheet, on Android 11 and later
  • Contacts (vCard and MeCard), calendar events and places are filled into the system apps' own forms, so QRMate never needs access to your contacts or your calendar
  • Email, SMS and phone numbers open ready to send or dial
  • Payment codes, including UPI requests and SEPA bank transfers, are shown in full before anything opens
  • Product barcodes show the check digit, the ISBN for books, and the country where the number was registered

Link Safety, Checked On Your Phone

A code can say one thing and point somewhere else. Before a link opens, QRMate warns you about:

  • plain http addresses
  • a bare IP address instead of a domain name
  • look-alike domains built from characters of another alphabet
  • shortened links that hide where they really lead
  • the "real.site@fake.site" trick

Auto-open never opens a link that raised a warning. The check runs on the device, and the link is not sent anywhere to be checked.

Create Your Own Codes

  • More than 20 kinds: text, link, Wi-Fi, contact, email, SMS, phone, payment request, location, calendar event, and barcodes such as Code 128 and EAN-13
  • Pick the code and background colours, with a contrast check that tells you when a pair will not scan
  • Put a logo or an icon in the middle
  • Set the size and the quiet margin around the code
  • Every code is read back before it is shown to you, so you know it scans
  • Share it, or save it as a PNG

History

  • Two tabs, one for codes you scanned and one for codes you made
  • Search, favourites, and swipe or multi-select to delete, with Undo
  • Scanning the same code again moves it to the top instead of filling the list with copies, and Settings can keep the duplicates if you prefer
  • Export to CSV, into a file you choose

Your Codes Stay With You

Scans, created codes and history are kept in a private database on your phone. There is no account and no sign-in, and nothing you scan or create is uploaded. The camera is used only while the scanner is open; frames are read on the device and never stored.

QRMate is paid for by ads, and asks for your consent where the law requires it. You can change that choice at any time in Settings.

GPS Map Camera

Stamp address, date and time on your photos.

Take photos with the address, coordinates, date and time printed on them, or stamp a photo you already have. Useful for site visits, field work and reports.

What it does

GPS Map Camera takes a photo and prints where and when it was taken right onto it, so the picture carries its own record.

What Goes On The Photo

  • The address, worked out from your location
  • Latitude and longitude, in decimals or in degrees, minutes and seconds
  • Date, day and time, with the time zone
  • Optionally the altitude, the accuracy in metres, the compass heading and a Plus Code
  • A place block with a pin and the name of the place, drawn by the app itself

Make The Stamp Yours

  • Several templates, from a slim one-line strip to a full card
  • Six box colours, adjustable transparency and text size
  • A switch for every field, so the stamp says only what you need
  • Add a note, your name, and your company name or logo for site reports
  • A live preview while you choose, so there are no surprises

Take One, Or Stamp One You Have

Use the camera, or stamp a photo already on your phone. The place can come from the photo's own details, from where you are now, or from an address you type in yourself.

Built For Field Work

  • Project folders, so each site's photos stay together
  • A gallery grouped by date and place
  • The location is written into the photo's own details (EXIF) too, so any gallery can show where it was taken. You can switch that off and keep only the printed stamp

Your Location, Used For One Thing

Location is read only while the camera or the stamp screen is open, never in the background, and no location history is kept. Your photos and your position are not uploaded: everything, including the place block, is drawn on your phone. There is no account and no sign-in.

GPS Map Camera is paid for by ads, and asks for your consent where the law requires it. You can change that choice at any time in Settings.
