Privacy Policy

GPS Map Camera · package deepbrain.lab.gpscamera · last updated 22 September 2026

GPS Map Camera is published by DeepBrain LAB. It takes photos stamped with where and when they were taken. This policy explains, in plain words, what the app does and does not do with your information, and above all with your location.

Your location

  • Location is used only to print the place on the photos you take (the address, the coordinates and, if you turn it on, a small map).
  • It is read only while the camera or the "Stamp a photo" screen is open. The app never reads your location in the background and does not have the background location permission.
  • No location history is kept. The only place your location is stored is inside the photos you take: in the visible stamp and in the photo's own GPS tags (EXIF), so gallery apps such as Google Photos can show where it was taken.
  • You can refuse location. The app still works: photos are taken without a place, or with one you type yourself.

What stays on your phone

Your photos are saved on your phone in Pictures/GPS Map Camera (or, on Android 8 and 9, in a folder you pick). They are never uploaded to us, never shared and never sold. Your stamp settings, note, company name and logo are kept in the app's private storage. There is no account and no sign in, so we have no way to see any of it. A photo you choose to share with another app carries its stamp and its GPS tags, so whoever receives it can see where it was taken.

The business details you can add in Stamp settings (a company logo, company name, employee name and site or job ID) are kept only in the app's private storage on your phone and appear only on your own photos. They are never sent to us or to anyone else.

To save data and to work with no signal, the app keeps two small caches in its private cache folder: the map tiles it has drawn (at most about 20 MB) and the last few address lookups (at most 40 places). Settings → Clear map cache deletes both at once. Uninstalling the app deletes them too.

What leaves your phone

Address lookup

The coordinates of a new position are turned into an address by your phone's own geocoding service (Android's Geocoder, provided on most phones by Google as part of the system). The app sends nothing to us for this. Offline, the stamp shows the coordinates only, or an address already in the cache.

Map (optional)

Only while "Show map on photos" is switched on, the app downloads map images ("tiles") from MapTiler (MapTiler's privacy policy) to draw the small map on the photo. Each request names a tile by its zoom level and x/y number, which reveals the approximate area around you (about 600 m across), together with the app's own MapTiler key. Your photos, your address and your exact position are never sent. Tiles already in the cache are not requested again. With the switch off, no map request is made at all. Map data © MapTiler © OpenStreetMap contributors.

Ads

The app shows ads from Google AdMob. To fill an ad, Google's software may use your device's advertising ID and approximate, non-precise information about your device, such as its model, language and coarse region. We never send Google your photos or your location.

  • You can reset or delete the advertising ID in Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • In the EEA and the UK the app asks for consent before any personalised ad is shown, and the choice can be changed at any time from Privacy settings in the app's menu.
  • Google's own policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Permissions

  • Camera - to take the photos. Asked when you first open the camera, after an explanation. Without it you can still add a stamp to a photo already on your phone.
  • Location (precise or approximate, your choice) - to print the place on the photo, only while the camera or stamp screen is open. Asked when you first open the camera, after an explanation.
  • Internet - used by the ads software and, only if you switch the map on, for the map tiles.
  • No storage, contacts, phone or background location permission. Photos are chosen with the system photo picker and saved without any storage permission. The app contains no analytics and no crash reporting software.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and ad content is capped at a "Teen" rating.

Deleting your data

Everything the app stores is on your phone. You can delete it from inside the app, or uninstall the app to remove all of it at once. We hold no copy, so there is nothing on our side to delete.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version appears on this page with a new date.
