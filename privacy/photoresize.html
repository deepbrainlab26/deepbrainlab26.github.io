Privacy Policy

Photo Resizer & PDF Tools · package deepbrain.lab.photoresize · last updated 22 September 2026

Photo Resizer & PDF Tools is published by DeepBrain LAB. This policy explains, in plain words, what the app does and does not do with your information.

What stays on your phone

Everything you enter in the app is stored only in the app's own private storage on your device. It is never uploaded to us, never shared and never sold. There is no account and no sign in, so we have no way to see it. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

What leaves your phone

The app shows ads from Google AdMob. To fill an ad, Google's software may use your device's advertising ID and approximate, non-precise information about your device, such as its model, language and coarse region. We never send Google the content you create.

  • You can reset or delete the advertising ID in Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • In the EEA and the UK the app asks for consent before any personalised ad is shown, and the choice can be changed at any time from Privacy settings in the app's menu.
  • Google's own policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

The app opens only the photos and PDF files you choose yourself, through Android's own picker. It works on them on your phone and never uploads them.

Permissions

  • Internet - used only by the ads software. The app itself works with no connection.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and ad content is capped at a "Teen" rating.

Deleting your data

Everything the app stores is on your phone. You can delete it from inside the app, or uninstall the app to remove all of it at once. We hold no copy, so there is nothing on our side to delete.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version appears on this page with a new date.
