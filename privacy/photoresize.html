About this policy

This policy explains how Photo & PDF Resizer (a tool to resize and compress photos and to create, compress, merge and split PDF files), published on Google Play by DeepBrain LAB, handles your information. It covers the Android app with the package name deepbrain.lab.photoresize.

In short: Photo & PDF Resizer has no account and no sign-in, keeps what you create on your phone, and shows ads from Google AdMob. We, the developer, do not collect, store or see your personal information.

Who is responsible: DeepBrain LAB is the developer of the app and, where a law uses the term, the data controller for the little personal information we handle ourselves (such as the emails you send us). Our contact details are in the last section.

Information stored on your phone

The app keeps the following only on your phone, in its private storage:

  • Your settings and preferences
  • Temporary working copies while a file is being processed, in the app's private cache

This information is never uploaded to us, never shared by us and never sold. There is no account, so we have no way to see it.

Your photos and PDF files

  • The app opens only the photos and PDF files you pick, through Android's own photo picker and file picker. It cannot browse the rest of your storage and asks for no storage permission.
  • Every file is processed on your phone. Nothing is uploaded, and your original files are never changed.
  • The results are saved to your phone's shared storage (on Android 8 and 9, to a folder you pick once), where you can see, share or delete them like any other file.

Photo & PDF Resizer shows ads from Google AdMob. The services below may receive information directly from your phone. We (the developer) never receive it, and we never send them anything you create in the app.

Ads appear as banners, as native ads inside the app's screens and as full-screen ads between screens, and are always marked as ads. Google decides which ad is shown and acts as an independent controller of the information its ads software collects, under Google's Privacy Policy.

How information is used

  • To make the app work: everything you create is used only on your phone, to show it back to you.
  • To show ads: Google AdMob uses the information in section 4 to choose, show and measure ads. Where you have consented, ads can be personalised to your interests; otherwise Google shows non-personalised ads, which still use the device information needed for delivery, frequency capping and fraud prevention.
  • To answer you: if you email us, we use your email address and message only to reply.

We do not use your information for profiling, automated decisions with legal effects, or any purpose not listed here.

Legal bases

Where the law requires a legal basis (for example the EU and UK GDPR, Switzerland, Brazil's LGPD and India's DPDP Act), the processing described here relies on:

  • Consent for personalised ads and for storing or reading information on your device for advertising, asked for through Google's consent form. You can withdraw it at any time (see Your choices).
  • Legitimate interests for non-personalised ads, ad measurement and fraud prevention, which fund the free app.
  • Performance of a contract: providing the features you use, which happens on your own phone.

Sharing and selling

  • We do not sell your personal information for money, and we never give anyone what you create in the app.
  • We have no copy of what you create in the app, so there is nothing we could share or disclose.
  • The only information that leaves your phone goes directly to the services listed above, under their own policies.
  • Anything you choose to share yourself (for example with Android's Share button) goes to the app or person you choose. That is up to you, and the receiving app's policy applies.
  • If the law requires it, we would disclose only what we hold, which is at most the emails you send us.

How long data is kept

  • On your phone: until you delete it in the app, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app.
  • With Google: according to Google's own retention policy.
  • Emails you send us: only as long as needed to answer you, and never more than 12 months.

Security

  • Your data is kept in the app's private storage, which Android protects from other apps.
  • The app is excluded from Android's automatic cloud backup, so your data is not copied to any cloud without your action.
  • All network traffic, made by the ads software and the services listed above, uses encrypted HTTPS.
  • No method of storage or transfer is completely secure. Keeping your phone locked and up to date protects the data on it best.

Your choices and rights

Controls you have now

  • Ad personalisation: reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads (or Settings → Google → Ads on older phones).
  • Consent: where the law requires it (such as in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland) the app asks before any personalised ad is shown. Change your choice at any time from Privacy settings in the app's menu, which appears whenever a privacy choice applies where you live.
  • Permissions: allow or remove any permission in Android Settings → Apps → Photo & PDF Resizer → Permissions.
  • Delete everything: delete items in the app, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app. We hold no copy, so nothing is left on our side.

Your legal rights, wherever you live

Privacy laws around the world give you rights over your personal data, including the EU and UK GDPR, Switzerland's FADP, India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, Brazil's LGPD, Canada's PIPEDA, Australia's Privacy Act, Japan's APPI, South Korea's PIPA, Singapore's PDPA, South Africa's POPIA and US state laws such as California's CCPA. Depending on the law that applies to you, you may have the right to:

  • know what personal data is processed, and get a copy of it;
  • correct it, delete it, or move it to another service;
  • withdraw consent, and object to or restrict processing;
  • opt out of targeted advertising and of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal data;
  • nominate someone to act for you, and complain to your data protection authority.

You will never be treated differently for using these rights.

For residents of US states with privacy laws

In the last 12 months, the only personal information collected through the app was collected by Google's ads software, as described in section 4: identifiers (advertising ID, IP address), internet activity (interactions with ads) and approximate location. We do not sell personal information for money. Where personalised ads count as "sharing" or "targeted advertising", you can opt out by deleting your advertising ID or turning off ad personalisation in Android settings, and through Privacy settings in the app when it is offered in your state. We do not knowingly sell or share the personal information of anyone under 16.

Because your data never reaches us, most of these rights are fully in your hands on your phone. For data held by Google, use Google's privacy controls. For anything else, email us and we will reply within 30 days.

Children

The app is for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and ad content in the app is limited to a maximum rating of "Teen". If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help remove it. Where the law sets a higher age for consent to personalised ads (up to 16 in some countries), only the person with parental responsibility can give that consent.

International transfers

We do not transfer your data anywhere: it stays on your phone. Google and the other services listed above may process the information they receive on servers in other countries, protected by their own safeguards, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.
