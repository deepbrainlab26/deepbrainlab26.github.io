What stays on your phone

Our apps have no accounts and no sign in. Everything you create in them (notes, expenses, study plans, photos, scan history and so on) is stored only in the app's private storage on your device. We never upload it, never see it, never share it and never sell it.

What leaves your phone

The app shows ads from Google AdMob. To fill an ad, Google's software may use your device's advertising ID and approximate, non-precise information about your device, such as its model, language and coarse region. We never send Google the content you create.

  • You can reset or delete the advertising ID in Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • In the EEA and the UK the app asks for consent before any personalised ad is shown, and the choice can be changed at any time from the app's menu.
  • Google's own policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Children

Our apps are not directed at children under 13, and ad content is capped at a "Teen" rating.

Deleting your data

Because all data stays on your phone, you are always in control: clear it from inside the app, or uninstall the app to remove it completely. We hold no copy.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version appears on this page with a new date.
